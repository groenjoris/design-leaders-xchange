The problem

The questions that really matter have nowhere good to go

You're responsible for design at a senior level. The questions on your plate are rarely clear-cut, and rarely something your colleagues or your team can truly help you with — they're too close to it, or too far below it.

The places you'd normally turn to fall short. Conferences are hit-and-miss: most talks don't touch your work, and in the hallways you get cornered by someone looking for a job or selling a tool. Design communities and forums stay at the surface — loose opinions, little depth. And everywhere you're careful about what you share, because you never quite know who's listening in.

So you're often left alone with the questions that really matter. You wonder whether you're ahead of the curve or behind it. You base decisions on a hunch of how others are handling it. And the valuable conversations — deep, honest, between equals — rarely happen.

Our insight

The best insights come from peers — given the right setting

The most relevant insights come from peers at your level who have already solved the exact question you're facing, or are right in the middle of it, just like you.

But that conversation doesn't happen by itself. It takes the right people at one table, around the theme you're dealing with right now, in a setting where you can speak freely. That is precisely what we organise: a curated group, thoughtful matching and formats that push every conversation towards depth.

We emphatically don't claim to have all the answers — much of this is too new for that. The wisdom is at the table, with you and your peers. We make sure it comes out.

What you take home: relevance (exactly the topics you're dealing with right now), recognition (you're not the only one, and that's a relief), inspiration (you go home energised, with a broadened perspective) and solutions (you hear how peers have already tackled your challenge).

What the day looks like

One day, carefully orchestrated

The people in the room

Design leaders and design managers (UX, CX, product design) with serious projects and real responsibility — at large multinationals, strong brands and international agencies. We curate the group carefully, so you spend the whole day among true peers.

We match you with the right people

Small tables of at most four people, matched on theme and level. You pick the theme that speaks to you and join the people wrestling with the same thing — the right fellow travellers.

The formats — our strength

No speakers, no panels, no stage. The whole day is built on proven, tightly facilitated formats: pair breakouts, hop-on-hop-off theme tables and custom tables around your own challenge. Designing and facilitating these formats is Anne and Joris's craft — they make sure every conversation gets past the pleasantries, goes deep and delivers. So you go home with concrete insights instead of business cards.

Example themes

Themes we hear from design managers

These are examples of themes design managers have brought to us recently. The actual themes are decided together at the start of the day — it's about what's on your mind, and your peers', right now.

01

AI in the design process

How is AI changing your process, and how are others handling it? How do you deal with resistance, with frontrunners, and with constraints from legal and compliance?

02

AI in your product

How do you add AI to your products, as a feature or as a foundation? Which framework fits, and what are the pitfalls along the way?

03

Career framework

How do you build a career framework that helps designers grow and gives them footing — and that also holds up in conversations with HR and management?

04

Leadership

What kind of leader do you want to be? How do you give your team direction and claim your seat at the table, without losing yourself?

05

Moral ambition

How do you put design to work for things that truly matter — and how do you relate to choices your organisation makes that you struggle with?

06

Change management

How do you bring your organisation along in change — new ways of working, new structures, a different role for design?

Frequently asked questions

Good to know

Is this yet another conference or networking event?
No. No speakers, no stage, no random talks. One day of intensive conversation with a small, curated group of peers, at tables of at most four people.
Will I be approached by recruiters or vendors?
No. That's exactly what we rule out. No sponsors, no selling, no job offers — a safe environment among equals.
How confidential is it, really?
Completely off the record. We make no recordings and take no minutes. Everyone signs an NDA, so everyone can speak freely.
What if none of the themes fit me?
The themes are decided at the start of the day, based on what's on the participants' minds. And in the afternoon we form custom tables around your own challenge — so your theme always gets its turn.
How am I matched with other participants?
You pick the table with the theme that speaks to you, and we guard the composition: small, at the right level, with the right mix. Want to switch? Just hop to another table.
What does it cost and what's included?
Pricing will follow shortly. In any case, the venue, lunch and drinks are included. No hidden costs, no sponsors.
