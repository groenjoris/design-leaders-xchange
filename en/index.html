Sound familiar?

You lead designers in a large organisation. The questions on your plate rarely have a clear-cut answer, and the right approach isn't written in any book: our field is evolving fast. You don't want to reinvent the wheel, and you keep wondering: how are others handling this?

At conferences you sometimes meet peers who really move you forward, but those moments are rare. Much of the time goes to talks that have little to do with your work, to vendors, or to people looking for their next job. UX communities mostly focus on design itself, not on the challenges you face as a manager.

What you get out of it

Inspiration for your own solution

You learn what challenges other organisations in your field are dealing with, hear how peers have already tackled your question, and leave with concrete ideas to take back.

An honest outside view

Peers from outside your organisation look at your approach without stakes, politics or history. They know the problem first-hand and tell you what they see. That often puts you on a new track.

A network of peers

The Mastermind takes place twice a year. People who click stay in touch, and every edition brings new peers and fresh perspectives.

A sharper view of your own approach

You help others think through their challenges and share what worked for you. Putting your experience into words shows you more clearly what makes your approach strong, and what doesn't.

Example themes

These are challenges we discussed with UX managers this year. The themes of the day are based on the intake calls; on the day itself, you decide where to focus your attention.

01

AI in the design process

How is AI changing your process, and how are others approaching it? How do you deal with resistance, with early adopters, and with constraints from legal and compliance?

02

AI in your product

How do you bring AI into your products, as a feature or as a foundation? Which framework fits, and what are the pitfalls along the way?

03

Leadership

How do you lead now that the way we work is changing, not least because of AI? What do you steer on when tasks, roles and pace are shifting, and how do you bring your team along?

04

Career framework

Mature UX organisations have a career framework. They come in all shapes and sizes, each with its own pros and cons. What are you running into in your situation, and what can you learn from other systems?

05

Change management

Reorganisations, mergers, new ways of working: UX is always on the move. How do you bring your teams along into new structures and processes, and perhaps into a different role for design?

Who it's for

For design managers (UX, CX, product design) with substantial projects and real responsibility, at large organisations, strong brands and international agencies. Each Mastermind brings together a group of 12 to 16 participants.

At a Mastermind, the people around the table are everything. That's why we put the group together carefully in advance, which also means not everyone can take part.

  • The Mastermind is for managers who lead designers rather than design themselves.
  • We bring together UX managers from different industries, so there are no competitors at the table.
  • We make sure there is enough common ground in the challenges, so everyone learns as much as possible from each other.
  • This Mastermind is held in English, for international UX managers working in the Netherlands. Speak Dutch? There is also a Dutch-language session on 29 January.

About us

How it started

We, Anne Vroegop and Joris Groen, have been working together in UX since 2014.

At the end of 2025 we organised our first Mastermind on AI and UX, mainly to gather inspiration for our training programmes. AI is moving so fast that some questions already had a different answer a few months later. What lasted was the value of the day itself: spending a full day sharing experiences with senior UX people in a tight format produced far more insight and inspiration than the average conference.

We then refined the formats and asked our network what they needed. This format is the result.

Anne Vroegop

Anne is a design leader and facilitator with many years of experience guiding teams and leaders in the design world, through her UX Academy and Human AI Academy. She is at her best helping a group of professionals get to the heart of the matter quickly, using formats that combine sharpness with openness.

Frequently asked questions

Will recruiters or vendors approach me?
No. That's exactly what we rule out. No sponsors, no sales pitches, no job offers: just a safe space among peers.
How confidential is it, really?
Completely off the record. Nothing is recorded and no minutes are taken. Everyone signs an NDA, so everyone can speak freely.
What if none of the themes fit me?
We set the themes based on a personal intake with each participant. During the day you choose which themes to focus on, and in the afternoon we form tables around your own challenge. So your topic always gets its turn.
What if I can't make it after all?
Cancellation is possible in some cases. If something unexpected comes up, let us know as soon as possible and we'll work out a solution together.
