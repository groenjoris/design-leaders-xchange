What is it?

A day of open, in-depth conversation with a small group of design managers, kindly hosted by Seven Peaks in central Bangkok.

You step outside your own organisation for a day and put your challenges on the table with peers who are in the middle of them too, or have already found their way through.

No presentations, no case studies. Structured formats help you get from challenge to practical answers quickly.

Sound familiar?

You manage a team of designers. Your challenges are rarely clear-cut, and because the field is changing so fast, the answers aren't in any book. How are others tackling this? Conferences and UX communities rarely tell you: they mostly focus on design, or on cases far removed from your own practice.

What you get out of it

Inspiration

What challenges are other organisations and design managers facing? Bring your own case, get inspired by how peers have already tackled your challenge, and take concrete ideas back with you.

An honest outside view

Peers from outside your organisation look at your approach without stakes, politics or history. They know the problem first-hand and share what they see, candidly but constructively. That often puts you on a new track.

Broaden your network

You meet design managers from other organisations in Bangkok, and the group stays in touch after the day.

See where you stand

How far along are other organisations? Putting your approach next to that of your peers shows you where you're ahead and where you can still learn. That gives you confidence in your choices.

Our themes

We set the themes together at the start of the day, based on what's on everyone's mind; then you decide where to focus your attention.

AI in the design process

How is AI changing your process, and how are others approaching it? How do you deal with resistance, with early adopters, and with constraints from legal and compliance?

AI in your product

How do you bring AI into your products, as a feature or as a foundation? Which framework fits, and what are the pitfalls along the way?

Leadership

How do you lead now that the way we work is changing, not least because of AI? What do you focus on when tasks, roles and pace are shifting, and how do you bring your team along?

Change management

Reorganisations, mergers, new ways of working: UX is always on the move. How do you bring your teams along into new structures and processes, and perhaps into a different role for design?

Who it's for

For design managers (UX, CX, product design) with substantial projects and major responsibilities.

  • The day is moderated in English, but it's well suited to non-native speakers. It's all about the conversations between participants, not with the moderators, so feel free to talk in Thai at your table.
  • At a Mastermind, the people around the table are everything. That's why we put the group together carefully in advance, which also means not everyone can take part.
  • The Mastermind is for managers who lead designers rather than design themselves.
  • We bring together UX managers from different industries, and we avoid putting direct competitors at the same table.
  • We make sure there is enough common ground in the challenges, so everyone learns as much as possible from each other.
  • We keep the group small enough for depth and large enough for variety: 12 to 16 participants.

About us

How it started

We, Anne Vroegop and Joris Groen, have been working together in UX since 2014.

At the end of 2025 we organised our first Mastermind on AI and UX, here in Bangkok, mainly to gather inspiration for our training programmes. AI is moving so fast that some questions already had a different answer a few months later. What lasted was the value of the day itself: spending a full day sharing experiences with senior UX people in a tight format produced far more insight and inspiration than the average conference.

We then refined the formats and asked our network what they needed. This format is the result.

Anne Vroegop

Anne is a design leader and facilitator with many years of experience guiding teams and leaders in the design world, through her UX Academy and Human AI Academy. She is at her best helping a group of professionals get to the heart of the matter quickly, using formats that combine sharpness with openness.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a sales or coaching event?
No. There's nothing to buy, no coaching programme and no sales follow-up. It's a day of conversation between peers, with us as moderators.
Can I see the list of participants in advance?
We don't publish a list of participants, but we do share it before the Mastermind.
Will recruiters or vendors approach me?
No. That's exactly what we rule out. No sponsors, no sales pitches, no job offers: just a safe space among peers.
How confidential is it, really?
Completely off the record. Nothing is recorded, no minutes are taken and nothing is attributed to anyone, so everyone can speak freely.
Can you cater for dietary requirements?
Yes. Let us know in advance what you can and can't eat, and we'll take it into account when arranging lunch.
